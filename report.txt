                              EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

NetMonitor is a web-based Network Safety Monitoring Tool designed to combine 
the packet inspection depth of Wireshark with the active scanning and 
reconnaissance power of Nmap. It is a DEFENSIVE cybersecurity application 
built for beginners with a modern dark-themed UI.

Primary Goal: Provide real-time network monitoring, threat detection, and 
security alerts with plain English explanations suitable for users without 
deep cybersecurity expertise.
